Task Description

A pole is balanced on top of a cart. The agent must move the cart left or right to keep the pole upright. The episode ends if the pole tilts beyond ±90° or the cart hits the boundary.

🎯 Reward
  • Each survival frame: +0.1
  • Pole falls or cart out of bounds: -10, episode ends
🧭 State
  • cartX: cart center X position (0 ~ 600)
  • cartVelX: cart horizontal velocity (approx. -10 ~ 10)
  • poleAngle: pole tilt angle in radians (-1.57 ~ 1.57)
  • poleAngularVel: pole angular velocity (approx. -6 ~ 6)
🕹️ Actions
  • 0: none (no force)
  • 1: left (push left)
  • 2: right (push right)
⚙️ Suggested Parameters

Beginner

  • bins = 6 (default)
  • ε = 0.3, α = 0.5, γ = 0.95
  • High-dimensional state — needs more episodes

Advanced

  • Enable DQN, observe distillation
  • Adjust bins to see discretization effects
  • R² sync rate above 0.8 is ideal
