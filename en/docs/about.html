🚀 About Rein Room

Rein Room is a reinforcement learning (RL) experimental platform specifically designed for the browser environment. Our mission is to transform abstract and complex AI training processes into a visual, actionable, and observable interactive experimental space, allowing learners to witness the full journey of an AI—from random exploration to policy convergence—directly in the browser without installing any extra software.

In Rein Room, users can create agents, configure learning parameters, observe reward curves, analyze changes in Q-Tables or neural network weights, and monitor learning status in real-time through various charts. We emphasize "task-oriented learning" and "evolution through trial and error," helping learners understand that reinforcement learning is not just a collection of formulas, but a process of optimizing decisions through interaction with the environment.

The platform's core engines (such as reinforceEngine.js and dqnWebWorker.js) are originally developed with education as the primary focus. Through a graphical interface and real-time interactive design, we aim to lower the entry barrier for reinforcement learning, enabling students and self-learners to gain a more intuitive understanding of core concepts like Exploration, Exploitation, and Long-term Reward.
